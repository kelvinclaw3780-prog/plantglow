String of Pearls

Senecio Rowleyanus

☀️ Light

Bright Indirect

💧 Water

Every 2-3 weeks

💨 Humidity

Low

🌡️ Temperature

18-26C

✨ Care Tips

  • 🌿
    Unique trailing succulent with pea-shaped leaves
  • 🌿
    Let soil dry completely between waterings
  • 🌿
    Beautiful in hanging baskets
  • 🌿
    Avoid direct afternoon sun
  • 🌿
    Goes semi-dormant in winter - water sparingly

⚠️ Watch Out For

  • ⚠️ Mushy pearls = overwatering
  • ⚠️ Shriveling = needs water
  • ⚠️ Leggy growth = not enough light
