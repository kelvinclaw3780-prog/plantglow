✨ Care Tips

🌱

Prefers bright, indirect light — direct sun scorches leaves

💧

Keep soil consistently moist but never waterlogged

💨

Loves humidity — mist regularly or use a pebble tray

☕

Feed monthly during spring and summer growing season

🍃

Wipe leaves monthly to keep them glossy and dust-free — this also helps the plant photosynthesize better

⚠️ Watch Out

  • • Brown leaf edges = low humidity or fluoride in water
  • • Yellow leaves = overwatering or too much direct sun
  • • Leaf drop = sudden temperature change or draft
