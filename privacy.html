Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

PlantGlow is operated by Tradex Dev. Co. ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect and use your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Registration: When you register, we collect your WhatsApp phone number (used for login authentication via a verification code) and your chosen display name.

Saved Favorites: When you are logged in, your saved plant favorites are stored in our database, linked to your account. This allows you to access your favorites across all devices.

Plant Ratings: When you rate plant care tips as "helpful" or "not helpful," your vote is recorded in our database. Votes are anonymous and aggregated — individual votes are not displayed publicly.

Email (Optional): If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email to send service updates and promotions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account via WhatsApp verification code
  • Sync your saved favorites across all your devices
  • Record and aggregate plant tip ratings
  • Send service updates and promotions (if subscribed)
  • Improve PlantGlow content and features

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers operated by Railway (our application hosting provider) and Turso (our database provider). These services are cloud-based and may store data internationally.

We also use Firebase (by Google) for authentication and analytics, and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. These services collect anonymous usage data.

4. Cookies

Essential cookies: Required for the site to function — e.g., remembering your cookie consent preference. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use PlantGlow. You can decline these in the cookie consent banner.

5. Data Sharing

We DO NOT:

  • Share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes
  • Sell or rent your contact information
  • Display your individual votes publicly

We share minimal anonymous data with Firebase and Google Analytics for service improvement purposes only.

6. Data Retention

Your favorites and vote records are retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, this data is removed upon request. Contact us at plantglownet@gmail.com to request data deletion.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of analytics cookies via our cookie banner

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at plantglownet@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

PlantGlow is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at plantglownet@gmail.com
